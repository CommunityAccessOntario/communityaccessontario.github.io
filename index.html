THE COALITION

Public schools. Public decisions. Public accountability.

An independent public-interest initiative examining how decisions affecting community access to school facilities, public spending and institutional accountability are made within the York Region District School Board (YRDSB).

About this site

This is an evolving research record, not a statement of proven misconduct. We distinguish documented facts, institutional positions, analysis and unanswered questions. Research pages will be updated as primary-source evidence is verified.

Our approach

We seek original documents, identify the decision-makers and legal or policy authority involved, compare official explanations against the public record, and publish specific questions that remain unanswered.

INVESTIGATION 01

Governance & Transparency

How have YRDSB's decision-making processes, delegated authorities and public reporting practices changed over time?

Research framework · Historical claims pending documentary verification

The central question

Which decisions require an explicit trustee vote, which may be taken under delegated administrative authority, and which are subsequently presented to trustees for information only?

Proposed historical investigation

Earlier records · circa 2000 onward

Establish a baseline using historical Board and committee minutes, policies, procedures and approval reports. Were comparable decisions submitted for trustee approval?

Changes in delegated authority

Identify dated amendments to governance policies, delegation instruments and administrative procedures. Compare the exact language before and after each change.

Approval versus information

Track whether reports concerning comparable matters changed from recommendations for approval to information-only presentations. Determine whether any change was authorized by trustees.

2025–2026 · Community Use case study

Reconstruct the chain from the original fee-related Board decision to subsequent implementation, including who authorized each step and what trustees were told.

Present-day public access

Evaluate whether members of the public can reconstruct proposals, recommendations, decisions, votes and implementation from available records.

Decision traceability test

ProposalRecommendationAuthorityDecision / voteImplementation

Each stage will be linked to an actual record. An unlocated document will be labelled “not located,” not “does not exist.”

Questions for YRDSB

  • What formal delegation permits administrative changes to fees or other material conditions?
  • Which trustee-approved policies and resolutions govern that delegation?
  • Which meeting minutes, reports and supporting documents are publicly accessible?
  • How can the public inspect records that are not published online?
  • How does YRDSB distinguish items requiring approval from items presented for information?

Evidence register

To add: historical Board minutes; current and archived governance policies; committee reports; delegation documents; relevant Education Act provisions; correspondence and responses.

INVESTIGATION 02

Facility Fees

How were YRDSB Community Use of Schools rates calculated, approved and implemented, and what is their effect on community access?

Active research · Quantitative claims require source-linked tables

Lines of inquiry

  • Approval and authority: what trustees voted on, what administration implemented, and what legal or policy authority applies.
  • Rate changes: compare dated fee schedules and representative permits, including ancillary charges.
  • Cost justification: identify the actual cost components used to establish rates and the evidence supporting them.
  • Provincial funding: examine Ministry support for Community Use and how it is reflected in YRDSB accounting.
  • Facility availability: investigate utilization, unused capacity and the implications for public access.
  • Cross-board comparison: compare like-for-like school facilities and user categories across Ontario boards.
  • Permit administration: cancellation rules, partial cancellations, revision fees and billing for unused bookings.

Questions for YRDSB

Where is the precise authorization for each rate change? What methodology was used? What costs are recovered through fees, and how is provincial funding accounted for? How are community groups affected?

Evidence register

To add: dated fee schedules, February 25, 2025 Community Use report, Board decisions, actual permit invoices, Ministry funding documents, utilization records and written YRDSB explanations.

INVESTIGATION 03

Non-Union Compensation & Mercer / 211 Jobs

A review of job evaluation, market benchmarking, classification and compensation decisions for non-union positions.

Research framework · Figures and classifications to be checked against source documents

Four mechanisms to distinguish

  1. Market benchmarking: Mercer methodology, comparison employers, benchmarks and conclusions.
  2. Job evaluation and rebanding: how positions were assessed and moved between salary bands.
  3. Movement within salary grids: rules and costs of progression within a band.
  4. General compensation increases: across-the-board adjustments and the authorities approving them.

Key questions

  • What is the documented scope of the 211 job classifications under review?
  • Who commissioned, reviewed and approved Mercer-related work?
  • What were the total and incremental financial implications?
  • Which recommendations required trustee approval?
  • Were potential conflicts of interest disclosed and managed according to policy?

Evidence register

To add: Mercer-related reports, classification tables, salary band schedules, Board and committee minutes, budgets, approval documents and conflict-of-interest policies.

INVESTIGATION 04

Facility-User Insurance

What changed in insurance arrangements for community users of school facilities, and how were responsibilities, premiums and implementation decisions determined?

Active research · Provincial and board-specific decisions must be separated

Research areas

  • OSBIE and the IRC Facility User Insurance portal: provincial rollout versus YRDSB-specific requirements.
  • Historical management of Community Use, insurance and risk responsibilities.
  • Changes in portfolio responsibility following Jeff Fair's departure and the subsequent role of Janna Lagaev.
  • Premiums, coverage requirements, user communication and administrative processes.
  • Procurement, decision authority and documented reasons for changes.

Questions

Which changes were mandated by the insurer or provincial system, and which were discretionary YRDSB choices? What is the documentary trail for those choices?

Evidence register

To add: OSBIE/IRC documentation, dated YRDSB user guidance, insurance requirements, relevant Board records and correspondence.

INVESTIGATION 05

KIV Group & eBase Software

An examination of the procurement, contracts, spending, administration and governance of relevant YRDSB technology services.

Scoping stage · No conclusions about wrongdoing

Questions to investigate

  • What services and systems were supplied by KIV Group and eBase Software, and during which periods?
  • Which legal entities signed contracts, and what were the contract terms and renewals?
  • What procurement method was used, and were competing bids sought?
  • What amounts were approved, paid and budgeted?
  • Who evaluated, authorized and supervised the procurement and implementation?
  • What vendor relationships, subcontracting arrangements or ownership links are documented?

Evidence register

To add: contracts, purchase orders, tender records, Board reports, vendor disclosures, payments and official responses. No unverified association should be presented as established fact.
